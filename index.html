See what your phone can sense

Nmind Lab uses your front camera to find your face and eyes, then drives the shapes from what it sees.

  • Look away and the shapes go quiet. Look back and they resume.
  • A second face pauses the session.
  • Blinks are counted. Calibrate to see a rough gaze point.

Everything runs on this phone. No video or images are saved or sent anywhere, and nothing is tracked.

Keep your head still and follow the dot with your eyes.
