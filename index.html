Prototype · focus training

Train calm focus

Nmind reads your eyes with the front camera. Hold the ring on the center with your eyes alone and the geometry grows richer, more detailed and more luminous. Let it drift and the shapes loosen and dim.

  • Calibrate once: keep your head still and follow the dot with your eyes.
  • Rest your eyes on the center target and breathe slowly.
  • Look away or let a second face in and it pauses.

Everything runs on this phone. No video or images are saved or sent anywhere, and nothing is tracked.
